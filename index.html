Privacy Policy

Bhailog Fantasy League (BFL) · Last updated 2 September 2026

BFL has no servers, no accounts and no database. Nothing you do in this app is sent to us, because there is nothing to send it to. Everything below is detail on that one fact.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no backend, no analytics service and no crash reporter. We cannot see who uses BFL, how often, or what they do in it.

What the app stores on your device

WhatWhereWhy
Premier League session tokeniOS Keychain, marked this device only so it is excluded from iCloud backupsKeeps you signed in so you can save lineups and transfers
Your team idApp storage on the deviceIdentifies the team the app shows
Your settingsApp storage on the deviceTheme, reminders, haptics and similar preferences
Cached Fantasy Premier League dataApp storage on the devicePlayers, fixtures and scores, so the app is fast and uses less data

All of it is removed when you delete the app. Signing out from Settings → Your team → Sign out removes the session token immediately.

Your Premier League password

BFL never receives it. The app has no password field. When you sign in, BFL opens Premier League's own sign-in page at account.premierleague.com in a browser view, with the address shown on screen, and you type your password there. BFL receives only the resulting authorisation code and exchanges it for a session token.

We could not store your password even if we wanted to: Premier League's identity provider supports no method that would accept one from an app like this.

Where your data goes

The app talks to two places, both operated by the Premier League:

  • fantasy.premierleague.com — to read your team, players, fixtures, leagues and scores, and to save lineup or transfer changes you make
  • account.premierleague.com — to sign you in and to renew your session

Requests go straight from your device. They are not proxied through us, and we operate nothing in between. Your use of those services is governed by the Premier League's own privacy policy and terms.

Tracking and advertising

BFL does not track you. There is no advertising SDK in the app, no advertising identifier is read, and no data is shared with anyone for advertising or any other purpose. If advertising is ever added, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and any tracking would require your explicit permission through iOS's own prompt.

Notifications

Deadline reminders are scheduled entirely on your device from the fixture list the app has already downloaded. There is no push server and no push token. The reminders are off until you turn them on.

Face ID and Touch ID

If you enable the app lock, the check is performed by iOS. Biometric data never leaves Apple's Secure Enclave, and BFL only learns whether the check succeeded.

Children

BFL is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. To remove everything the app holds, delete the app. To revoke BFL's access to your Fantasy Premier League account, sign out in the app, or manage it from your Premier League account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
