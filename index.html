Privacy Policy

Bhailog Fantasy League (BFL) · Last updated 3 September 2026

BFL has no servers, no accounts and no database. Nothing you do in this app is sent to us, because there is nothing to send it to. Everything below is detail on that one fact.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no backend, no analytics service and no crash reporter. We cannot see who uses BFL, how often, or what they do in it.

Your Premier League password

There are two ways to sign in, and they handle your password differently. Read this section rather than assuming, because an earlier version of this policy said BFL had no password field, and that is no longer true.

Premier League's own page. Choosing Continue to Premier League, or one of the Google, Apple, Facebook or X buttons, opens Premier League's sign-in page at account.premierleague.com with the address shown on screen. You type your password there and BFL never sees it. This is the only route that works for an FPL account opened through one of those providers, and the only acceptable one for those, because the password being typed belongs to Google or Apple rather than to FPL.

BFL's own form. The email and password fields on the sign-in screen are BFL's. What happens to what you type is that the app loads Premier League's authorisation page in a hidden browser view on your device, submits your details to it, and reads back the resulting authorisation code. So the password does pass through the app. It does not pass through us: BFL operates no server, so there is no address it could be sent to and no log it could appear in.

If you tick Remember me, your password is written to your device's own secure credential store — the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore — encrypted by the operating system, readable only while the device is unlocked, and excluded from iCloud and Google backups so it cannot travel to another device. It is kept when you sign out, on purpose, so that coming back is one tap. Signing in again with Remember me unticked erases it. Leaving it unticked stores your email address only.

Storing a password is a real trade rather than an oversight, so it is worth being plain about the risk: unlike a session token, a password is not scoped or revocable, and people reuse passwords. If you would rather BFL never held yours, use Premier League's own page instead, or leave Remember me unticked.

Where your data goes

The app talks to two places, both operated by the Premier League:

  • fantasy.premierleague.com — to read your team, players, fixtures, leagues and scores, and to save lineup or transfer changes you make
  • account.premierleague.com — to sign you in and to renew your session

Requests go straight from your device. They are not proxied through us, and we operate nothing in between. Your use of those services is governed by the Premier League's own privacy policy and terms.

Tracking and advertising

BFL does not track you. There is no advertising SDK in the app, no advertising identifier is read, and no data is shared with anyone for advertising or any other purpose. If advertising is ever added, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and any tracking would require your explicit permission — through iOS's App Tracking Transparency prompt, or an equivalent consent screen on Android.

Notifications

Deadline reminders are scheduled entirely on your device from the fixture list the app has already downloaded. There is no push server and no push token. The reminders are off until you turn them on.

Face, fingerprint and device unlock

If you enable the app lock, the check is performed by your device's operating system, not by BFL. Biometric data never leaves the secure hardware that holds it — Apple's Secure Enclave on iOS, the Trusted Execution Environment or secure element on Android — and BFL only learns whether the check succeeded.

Children

BFL is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. To remove everything the app holds, delete the app. To revoke BFL's access to your Fantasy Premier League account, sign out in the app, or manage it from your Premier League account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
